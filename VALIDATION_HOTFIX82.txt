Hotfix82 / Visual Palette V2.4.1 targeted validation
===================================================
Scope: offline audit only. Formal Palette Studio remains unchanged.

Reason for V2.4.1
-----------------
HF81 V2.4 multi-file audit exposed one recurring boundary case:
very high-L* blue-/violet-tinted whites can sit just outside the fixed
Tinted White envelope while being visually almost identical (CIEDE2000
< 3) to Tinted White samples in the same QTX.

V2.4.1 rule
------------
Keep all V2.4 rules unchanged. After the initial V2.4 classification,
perform ONE non-recursive bridge pass:
- candidate L* >= 90
- candidate C* <= 18
- candidate is not already Neutral Field
- the same dataset contains an INITIAL Tinted White with CIEDE2000 < 3
If all are true, promote the candidate to Neutral Field as
"Tinted White Bridge". Promoted samples cannot recruit more samples.
