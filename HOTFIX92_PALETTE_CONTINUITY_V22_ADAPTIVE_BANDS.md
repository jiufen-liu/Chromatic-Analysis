# Hotfix92 · Palette Continuity V2.2 Adaptive Bands
Diagnostic-only candidate. Formal Palette Studio behaviour remains frozen.

V2.1 multi-QTX validation showed excellent local ΔE00 numbers but excessive fixed
L*/C* subgroup fragmentation on sparse and medium datasets. V2.2 removes those
hard subgroup bins from layout. Broad hue family is the only hard skeleton;
CIEDE2000 is primary, tone/chroma/hue continuity stabilises appearance, spectral
RMS is secondary, Neutral follows L*, and only true perceptual outliers break rows.
No customer, product, colour-name, brand or filename special cases exist.
