Chromatic Analysis v0.14.6.4 - ColorCardPlan Selection + Fluorescent Preview Hotfix 12

Only two requested areas were changed in qtx_app/main_window.py:
1) Color-card plan selection
   - Ctrl+A is handled at the application event-filter level for the active palette window.
   - Select-all explicitly selects every non-empty card slot and refreshes custom card borders once.
   - Shift-click selects a continuous range.
   - Ctrl+Shift-click appends a continuous range.
   - Existing native Ctrl-click and Ctrl-drag behaviour remains in QListWidget.
2) Fluorescent colour-card screen preview
   - Only fluorescent cards inside ColorCardPlanWindow use a vivid clipped-sRGB preview.
   - Fluorescence detection, reflectance, measured Lab, delta-E, CPX slot/fold-corner logic and saved data are unchanged.

No other source file was edited.
